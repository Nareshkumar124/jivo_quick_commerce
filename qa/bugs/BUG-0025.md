## Steps to reproduce
1. QA rider R1 (+919000000103) online at QA-02; QA customer A (+919000000101) places a COD order; R1 accepts, picks up and marks arrived at the customer (task AT_CUSTOMER, order OUT_FOR_DELIVERY).
2. R1: `POST /api/v1/rider/tasks/{id}/cod-qr/` → 200 `{status: PENDING, amount: "190.00", expires_at: created + 20 s}`.
3. Wait past `expires_at`. R1: `GET /api/v1/rider/tasks/current/` → `cod_qr.status` **EXPIRED** (same `qr_id`). R1 does not ask for a new QR.
4. Send the signed webhook `qr_code.credited` for that `qr_id` with the full amount (19000 paise), a new payment id and event id.

## Expected
- 08 T10 (COD by UPI QR): "Any QR payment that is not needed (QR closed **or expired**, wrong amount, delivery failed) is refunded in full to the UPI payment automatically."
- 06 §2 slice 5.4: "Any other credit is refunded in full … (`cod_qr_unused`) …: a QR closed because cash was taken, **an expired QR**, a wrong amount, a delivery that failed."
- The module docstring says the same.

So the QR should not become PAID; one `Refund` (`cod_qr_unused`) should be created and ops alerted (`COD_QR_REFUNDED`).

## Actual
The webhook answers 200. The QR becomes **PAID** and no refund is created. R1 can then deliver with `mode: upi` and the order ends `DELIVERED` / `COD_COLLECTED`.
```
TC-92c: late credit on expired QR -> QR PAID, refunds []
AssertionError: a credit on an expired QR paid the order (06 slice 5.4: it must be refunded)
```
Read-only SQL (`delivery_codqr` joined to `orders_order`):
```
codqr 28, order 1005: status PAID, amount 190.00,
created_at 13:30:57.92, expires_at 13:31:17.81, paid_at 13:31:20.34 (2.5 s after expiry)
order payment_status COD_COLLECTED, status DELIVERED
```
The paths that *replace* an expired QR behave as documented. P5-TC-92b passes: asking again closes the old QR as EXPIRED, and a late payment on it is refunded once (`cod_qr_unused`). P5-TC-92d (a failed task) also passes.

## Risk
In the window between expiry and the late credit, the rider sees EXPIRED and may take cash.
- If the credit arrives **first**, `deliver` with cash is refused ("already paid by UPI"). The customer has then paid twice (cash in hand and UPI), and the system knows only one of the payments, so the rider must hand the cash back by hand.
- If the cash delivery comes first, the QR is closed and the late credit is refunded, which is correct.

## Possible root cause
`record_credit` (`apps/delivery/services/cod_qr.py` lines 261–268) checks `qr.status == PENDING` but not `qr.expires_at > now`. `display_status()` already treats a PENDING QR past `expires_at` as EXPIRED, but nothing changes the stored row until someone asks for a new QR.

## Suggested resolution
- Add `and qr.expires_at > now` to the acceptance condition, so the credit is refunded and the QR closed EXPIRED.
- Or, if Jivo wants to honour a payment made before `close_by` whose webhook arrives late, use the payment's own `created_at` from the gateway entity against `expires_at`, and update 06 / 08 T10.

Either way, add a test with an expired, unreplaced QR. QA's P5-TC-92c is the oracle for the documented rule.
